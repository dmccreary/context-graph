# Character Sheet: Nexus the Spider

The canonical identity document for Nexus, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Context Graph: How Organizations Use LLMs Cost Effectively** textbook.
Every pose prompt and every piece of AI-generated content involving this
character must re-anchor to the description below — it is the source of
truth for visual and voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Nexus
- **Species:** Spider
- **Subject:** Context graphs, enterprise AI, knowledge graphs, LLMs
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's trace the why!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Deep indigo/blue-purple — hex `#3949ab`
- **Accent color:** Warm orange — hex `#f57c00`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round glasses; clean and minimal
- **Expression:** Friendly, curious, inviting closed-mouth smile
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact, suitable for icon-sized display
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector, clean lines, transparent background

## Personality

- Curious and investigative — always asking "why?"
- Precise and methodical — weaves clean, logical webs of understanding
- Quietly enthusiastic — celebrates breakthroughs without being over-the-top
- Patient and supportive — understands enterprise AI is genuinely complex

## Voice

- Uses clear, technical language without unnecessary jargon
- Frames concepts through graph metaphors (nodes, edges, traces, connections)
- Refers to readers as "graph builders" or "context architects"
- Signature phrases: "Let's trace the why!", "Every node tells a story.", "Connect the dots — then ask why."

## Why This Mascot

A spider was chosen because its web is the most immediate natural metaphor
for a context graph — nodes connected by purposeful edges, built
incrementally, and used to capture what passes through. The indigo/orange
palette mirrors the textbook's theme, and the scholarly round glasses signal
precision and analytical thinking without making Nexus feel intimidating to
practitioners coming from non-graph backgrounds.
